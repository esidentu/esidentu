## Proof of builds

### 01 / Mbrace Africa

**A community and learning platform for people navigating a technology-driven world.**

Mbrace Africa is my founder build. It exists to help individuals and organizations navigate the changing relationship between careers, technology, and opportunity.

The problem I saw was simple: technology keeps changing what work requires, but access to clarity, practical learning, mentorship, and opportunity is still uneven. Mbrace is my response to that gap.

What it brings together:

- Career clarity for people making decisions about what comes next
- Practical technology capability for individuals and teams
- Mentorship, community, and access to opportunity
- Responsible learning around technologies changing work

What this build shows:

- I can move from problem observation to product direction
- I can design for people, not just features
- I can build a platform around learning, community, and access
- I can connect product thinking with real-world impact

[Visit Mbrace Africa](https://mbraceafrica.org/)  
[Explore Mbrace programs](https://mbraceafrica.org/programs)
